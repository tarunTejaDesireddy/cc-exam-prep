A. Integrity · B. Confidentiality · C. Availability · D. Non-repudiation

A. 21 · B. 22 · C. 23 · D. 25

**8.** A door unlocks when power fails. What is this configuration?
A. Fail-secure · B. Fail-safe · C. Fail-closed · D. A misconfiguration

A. At rest · B. In transit · C. In use · D. Archived

**10.** What is the first phase of incident response?
A. Detection · B. Containment · C. Preparation · D. Eradication

**11.** Backups are classified as which control function?
A. Preventive · B. Detective · C. Corrective · D. Deterrent

**12.** Which protocol resolves an IP address to a MAC address?
A. DNS · B. DHCP · C. ARP · D. ICMP

**13.** Which principle is violated when one person can both request and approve a payment?
A. Least privilege · B. Segregation of duties · C. Need to know · D. Defence in depth

**14.** Who determines the classification of a dataset?
A. The custodian · B. The data owner · C. The security team · D. IT operations

**15.** Business continuity is concerned with what?
A. Restoring systems after a disruption · B. Keeping functions running during a disruption · C. Backing up data · D. Insuring against loss

**16.** A control costs $40,000/year. The risk's ALE is $15,000. What should be recommended?
A. Implement it · B. Accept the risk, documented · C. Transfer the risk · D. Avoid the activity

**17.** Which is a **public** IP address?
A. 10.1.1.1 · B. 172.20.1.1 · C. 172.33.1.1 · D. 192.168.1.1

**18.** Which is attached to the object rather than the subject?
A. Capability list · B. Access control list · C. Clearance · D. Entitlement

**19.** MD5 is unsuitable for security use because:
A. Its output is too long · B. Practical collisions exist · C. It requires key exchange · D. It is too slow

**20.** Ransomware encrypts data with no exfiltration. Which CIA element is primarily affected?
A. Confidentiality · B. Integrity · C. Availability · D. None

**21.** What does an IDS do that an IPS does not?
A. Block traffic · B. Only detect and alert · C. Operate at layer 7 · D. Inspect encrypted traffic

**22.** Which document type is **not** mandatory?
A. Policy · B. Standard · C. Procedure · D. Guideline

**23.** Which is the FIRST step in securing an environment?
A. Deploy endpoint protection · B. Create an asset inventory · C. Enable MFA · D. Run a scan

**24.** A user has Secret clearance and is refused a Top Secret file. The author cannot override. Which model?
A. DAC · B. MAC · C. RBAC · D. Rule-based

**25.** SLE is calculated as:
A. AV × ARO · B. AV × EF · C. SLE × ARO · D. EF × ARO

**26.** Which malware spreads with no user action?
A. Virus · B. Trojan · C. Worm · D. Rootkit

**27.** What does a VPN protect?
A. Data at rest on the device · B. Data in transit between endpoints · C. The endpoint from malware · D. Traffic after the far endpoint

**28.** Privilege creep is caused by:
A. An attacker escalating rights · B. Rights accumulating over role changes · C. Shared accounts · D. Weak passwords

**29.** Which is an administrative control?
A. A firewall rule · B. Full-disk encryption · C. Security awareness training · D. A door lock

**30.** MTD is:
A. The target restore time · B. The maximum tolerable downtime · C. Acceptable data loss · D. Mean time to repair

**31.** Which of the four risk treatments takes risk to zero?
A. Mitigate · B. Transfer · C. Avoid · D. Accept

**32.** Which port does HTTPS use?
A. 80 · B. 143 · C. 443 · D. 8080

**33.** Access reviews are classified as:
A. Preventive · B. Detective · C. Corrective · D. Directive

**34.** Under IaaS, who patches the guest operating system?
A. The provider · B. The customer · C. Shared equally · D. The hypervisor

**35.** Which provides non-repudiation?
A. Symmetric encryption · B. A digital signature with the sender's private key · C. Hashing alone · D. A shared secret

**36.** Which is TRUE of an incident?
A. Every event is an incident · B. It actually or potentially jeopardises C, I or A · C. It requires data disclosure · D. It is always malicious

**37.** In a DMZ architecture, what must be prevented?
A. The internet reaching the DMZ · B. The DMZ freely reaching the internal network · C. Internal users reaching the internet · D. Administrators managing DMZ hosts

**38.** Which is NOT an authentication factor?
A. Something you know · B. Something you have · C. Something you are · D. Something you own

**39.** Segmentation primarily provides:
A. Prevention of intrusion · B. Limitation of lateral movement · C. Encryption between zones · D. Faster networks

**40.** Which describes a differential backup?
A. Everything, every time · B. Changes since the last backup of any type · C. Changes since the last full backup · D. Only deleted files

**41.** Who approves an information security policy?
A. The CISO · B. Senior management · C. Internal audit · D. The IT director

**42.** Degaussing is ineffective on:
A. Hard disk drives · B. Magnetic tape · C. Solid-state drives · D. Floppy disks

**43.** Which attack re-sends captured valid data?
A. On-path · B. Replay · C. Session hijacking · D. Spoofing

**44.** Which is the correct first canon of the ISC2 Code of Ethics?
A. Serve principals diligently · B. Protect society and the infrastructure · C. Act honourably · D. Advance the profession

**45.** A mantrap defends against:
A. Shoulder surfing · B. Tailgating · C. Dumpster diving · D. Phishing

**46.** Which is a possession factor?
A. A passphrase · B. A hardware token · C. A fingerprint · D. A security question

**47.** RPO is determined by:
A. Recovery site type · B. Backup frequency · C. Staff availability · D. Network bandwidth

**48.** Which cloud deployment model is shared by organisations with common requirements?
A. Public · B. Private · C. Hybrid · D. Community

**49.** Hashing provides:
A. Confidentiality · B. Integrity · C. Availability · D. Key exchange

**50.** An unpatched vulnerability differs from a zero-day because:
A. A patch exists but was not applied · B. It is less severe · C. It affects only old software · D. It is not exploitable

**51.** Which control is both physical and deterrent?
A. A biometric reader · B. Warning signage · C. A mantrap · D. Fire suppression

**52.** Residual risk is:
A. Risk before controls · B. Risk remaining after controls · C. Risk transferred to an insurer · D. Always zero

**53.** Which is the strongest wireless security protocol?
A. WEP · B. WPA · C. WPA2 · D. WPA3

**54.** Which describes ABAC?
A. The owner decides · B. The system uses labels · C. Access via job function · D. Multiple attributes evaluated at request time

**55.** Logging without monitoring results in:
A. Excessive storage costs · B. No detection capability · C. Regulatory breach · D. Slower systems

**56.** An emergency change should be:
A. Exempt from change control · B. Accelerated and documented retrospectively · C. Reverted afterwards · D. Avoided entirely

**57.** Which is TRUE of NAT?
A. It encrypts traffic · B. Its purpose is address conservation · C. It replaces a firewall · D. It resolves names
