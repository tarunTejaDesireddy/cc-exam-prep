A. Missing patches · B. No security training · C. A flood · D. A server room in a basement

A. Containment · B. Eradication · C. Recovery · D. Post-incident

**11.** Security awareness training is classified as:
A. Technical, preventive · B. Administrative, preventive · C. Administrative, detective · D. Physical, deterrent

A. ARP · B. DHCP · C. DNS · D. ICMP

A. Developing and documenting · B. Requesting and approving a payment · C. Monitoring and reporting · D. Training and assessing

A. The data owner · B. The data custodian · C. The data subject · D. Senior management

**15.** Disaster recovery is concerned with:
A. Keeping functions running during a disruption · B. Restoring systems after a disruption · C. Insuring against loss · D. Classifying data

**16.** A risk's ALE is $60,000. A control costing $25,000/year halves it. What should be recommended?
A. Reject it — the ALE is unchanged · B. Implement it — net benefit of $5,000 · C. Accept the risk · D. Transfer the risk instead

**17.** Which address indicates DHCP failure?
A. 10.0.0.1 · B. 127.0.0.1 · C. 169.254.4.7 · D. 192.168.0.1

**18.** Which answers "what may I access?"
A. An access control list · B. A capability list · C. A security label · D. A clearance

**19.** Which is TRUE of SHA-1?
A. It is the current standard · B. It is broken by practical collisions · C. It requires a key · D. It is reversible

**20.** Ransomware gangs exfiltrate data before encrypting. If a scenario mentions only encryption, which CIA element is affected?
A. Confidentiality · B. Integrity · C. Availability · D. Both A and C

**21.** An IDS is positioned out of band. What does this mean?
A. It can block traffic · B. It receives a copy of traffic and cannot interfere · C. It operates at layer 7 · D. It decrypts TLS

**22.** Which document contains numbered step-by-step instructions?
A. Policy · B. Standard · C. Procedure · D. Guideline

**23.** Shadow IT describes:
A. Approved but undocumented systems · B. Systems in use without IT or security's knowledge · C. Legacy systems · D. Backup infrastructure

**24.** Which model is most common in commercial organisations?
A. DAC · B. MAC · C. RBAC · D. ABAC

**25.** ALE is calculated as:
A. AV × EF · B. SLE × ARO · C. AV × ARO · D. EF × ARO

**26.** Which access control model grants access based on organisational role rather than owner discretion or system-wide labels?
A. DAC · B. RBAC · C. MAC · D. Rule-based

**27.** An infected laptop connects over VPN. What is the implication?
A. Encryption blocks the malware · B. The malware has an encrypted, authenticated path inward · C. The VPN quarantines it · D. No implication

**28.** Privilege escalation is:
A. Rights accumulating over role changes · B. An attacker gaining rights never granted · C. A role assignment · D. A provisioning process

**29.** Which is a technical control?
A. A background check · B. Full-disk encryption · C. A clean desk policy · D. Security training

**30.** Which comes first in continuity planning?
A. Site selection · B. The BIA · C. The DR plan · D. Buying hardware

**31.** Which treatment applies when a control costs more than the risk?
A. Mitigate · B. Avoid · C. Accept, documented · D. Transfer

**32.** Which port does LDAP use?
A. 389 · B. 443 · C. 636 · D. 3389

**33.** An audit log is which control function?
A. Preventive · B. Detective · C. Corrective · D. Deterrent

**34.** Under IaaS, who is responsible for the data?
A. The provider · B. The customer · C. Shared · D. Neither

**35.** Which of the following is the answer to tailgating?
A. A warning sign · B. A mantrap or access control vestibule · C. Stronger badge encryption · D. CCTV coverage

**36.** Which is TRUE of a breach?
A. Every incident is a breach · B. It requires data to have actually been disclosed · C. It must be deliberate · D. It applies only to encrypted data

**37.** Which trust level applies to a DMZ?
A. Untrusted · B. Semi-trusted · C. Trusted · D. Fully trusted

**38.** Which is a knowledge factor?
A. A proximity card · B. A PIN · C. An iris scan · D. A certificate

**39.** Microsegmentation differs from VLAN segmentation because it:
A. Is physical · B. Isolates individual workloads · C. Uses encryption · D. Operates at layer 1

**40.** Which backup type is fastest to create but slowest to restore?

**41.** What gives a security policy its authority?

**44.** Which canon covers acting legally?

**45.** Which physical control is corrective?
A. A bollard · B. A badge reader · C. Fire suppression · D. A warning sign

**46.** Which is TRUE of MFA using password plus SMS code?

**47.** Which is TRUE of RTO?
A. It measures data loss · B. It measures time to restore a function · C. It equals MTD · D. It is a hardware metric

**48.** Which BEST describes a KPI?
A. A metric warning that risk is increasing · B. A metric grading how well a process performs against a target · C. A one-time audit finding · D. A list of unpatched vulnerabilities

**49.** An employee moves from finance to marketing but keeps their finance system access. What is the correct fix?
A. Grant marketing access without touching finance access · B. Revoke the finance access that is no longer needed · C. Escalate to senior management · D. Do nothing, since it was granted legitimately

A. Being recently discovered · B. No patch existing · C. Affecting old software · D. Being unexploitable

**51.** Which is preventive and physical?
A. CCTV · B. A door lock · C. A warning sign · D. An audit log

A. The security team · B. Senior management · C. Internal audit · D. System owners in IT

A. It is slow · B. It is cryptographically broken · C. It lacks IPv6 support · D. It requires a RADIUS server

**54.** Rule-based access control differs from RBAC because it:
A. Uses job functions · B. Applies system-wide rules regardless of identity · C. Uses labels · D. Is owner-driven

**55.** Which is TRUE of logging?

**56.** Which BEST distinguishes segregation of duties from dual control?
